Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What areas does Vera cover?

Vera primarily serves Heidelberg and the surrounding Rhine-Neckar region, including Mannheim, Ludwigshafen, and surrounding towns. Arrangements throughout Germany and internationally can also be coordinated on request.

What languages does Vera speak?

Vera speaks English, German, Arabic, and Russian fluently, ensuring clear and comfortable communication with clients from around the world.

How quickly can I expect a response?

All inquiries receive a personal response within 24 hours. For urgent requests, please reach out via WhatsApp for the fastest possible assistance.

Is there a minimum budget for services?

Services are tailored to every budget. Whether you need a single restaurant reservation or a full week of curated experiences, Vera will work within your preferences to deliver exceptional results.

How does payment work?

After reviewing your request, Vera will provide a transparent quote outlining all costs. Payment terms and methods are discussed privately and handled with full discretion.

Can I request services for someone else?

Absolutely. Many clients arrange concierge services as gifts or for family members, colleagues, or guests visiting Heidelberg. Simply include the recipient's details in your request.
